Englive Privacy Policy

HantasAI Technologies · Effective date: 13 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Englive English-learning app ("Englive", "the app") uses, why, and what choices you have. Englive is published by HantasAI Technologies ("we", "us").

In short: your lessons, progress and voice recordings stay on your phone. We do not have user accounts and we do not run our own servers that collect your data. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information as described below.

1. Information stored only on your phone

  • Learning progress and scores. These are saved in the app's private storage on your phone so you can continue where you left off.
  • Settings. Examples are whether the Tamil meaning is shown, the teacher's voice and the speaking speed.
  • Your practice recording. When you use "record and compare", the app records your voice so you can play it back. Only your latest attempt is kept, in the app's temporary storage on your phone. It is never uploaded by the app and is replaced by your next recording.

We cannot see any of this information. Uninstalling the app deletes it. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the app's progress and settings in your phone's backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy.

2. Microphone and speech recognition

Englive asks for microphone permission so you can practise speaking. When you tap the microphone, the app uses your phone's built-in speech recognition to turn your speech into text and check your answer.

  • Where your phone supports it, recognition runs on the device.
  • Otherwise your phone's speech service (usually Google) may send the audio to its servers to recognise it. This is done by your phone's speech service, not by Englive, and is covered by that provider's privacy policy.
  • The app only listens after you tap the microphone. You can turn off microphone permission in your phone's settings at any time; the rest of the app keeps working.

3. Text-to-speech (the teacher's voice)

The teacher's voice is produced by your phone's text-to-speech engine (for example "Speech Services by Google"). The lesson text is passed to that engine on your phone. If you choose a voice marked "needs internet", the engine may send the text to its provider to generate the speech. Lesson text is course material, not personal information.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Englive is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating-system version, app version and language;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • ad interactions, such as ads shown and ads tapped.

Google's use of this information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads). If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice later in the app.

Ads need an internet connection. Lessons work fully offline, and no ads are shown when you are offline.

7. Security and retention

Information the app stores stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. It is kept until you delete the app's data or uninstall the app. Information collected by Google for ads is kept according to Google's policies.

8. Your rights

Because we do not hold your learning data, you control it directly. Clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to delete it. For questions about advertising data, use Google's controls described above, or contact us and we will help.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the app gains new features. We will change the effective date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or on the Play Store listing.
